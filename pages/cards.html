Notice: Demonstration Art

Welcome to Atua TCG! Please note that all images and artwork displayed on this site are for demonstration purposes only.

Original illustrations remain the property of their respective artists (including card artwork by Māori Mermaid). Cards without custom art feature symbolic element emblems to honour the living atua of Māori tradition.

Atua TCG is an unofficial, fan-made project — not affiliated with any iwi, hapū, or official cultural authority.
